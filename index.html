Who are we?

NAS3 is a society aiming to connect science students with people, opportunities, and experiences beyond their degrees. We want to make university life more than about just study, by building a community where curious students can learn from each other and enjoy the journey.

Why Join?

Networking Opportunities

Meet students across all science faculties and make connections that go beyond your degree. The friends you make here could become your future colleagues!

Events for Everyone

From BBQs to competitive trivia nights, we always have something on! Whether you want to unwind or get involved, NAS³ has you covered.

Industry Connections

Get one-on-one face time with professionals and alumni in science, research, and data fields through our Mixers and Industry Nights.

Resources & Study Materials

Access a library of past notes, study guides, and academic resources from past members, from research tips, to exam preparation. Everything your degree demands, all in one place!
